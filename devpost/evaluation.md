# ChangeGuard Evaluation Corpus & Accuracy Report

This document reports empirical performance and accuracy metrics for ChangeGuard across an evaluation corpus of 10 real, sanitized AI agent pull requests (generated by Claude 3.5 Sonnet and GPT-4o in production/open-source JS/TS repositories).

> **Disclosure:** The learner is the sole rater for this evaluation. To avoid cherry-picking biases, the two demo fixtures (`real-01: Express cart checkout` and `real-02: Auth middleware refactor`) were committed and locked *prior* to calculating aggregate precision metrics.

---

## 1. Evaluation Methodology

Each diff in the corpus was evaluated against:
1. **Deterministic Rules Tier (Client-side):**
   - **Rule 1 (R1):** Deleted test assertions with 1-to-1 same-file move matching.
   - **Rule 2 (R2):** Introduced empty or comment-only error handlers (`catch {}`, `.catch(() => {})`, etc.).
   - **Rule 3 (R3):** Skipped or focused tests (`it.skip`, `describe.only`, `xit`, `fit`, etc.).
2. **LLM Hypotheses Tier (Cited vs. Uncited):**
   - Synthesizes bug hypotheses citing lines actually sent in the prompt budget (`sentLineIds`).
   - Verified by the Citation Verifier client-side.
3. **Verification Ground Truth:**
   - Every flagged issue was manually verified against the full repository context and validated by writing or running a targeted Vitest/Jest reproduction test.
   - **True Positive (TP):** A finding that identifies an actual behavioral regression, dropped test coverage, or unhandled failure condition.
   - **False Positive (FP):** A finding where the change was an intentional refactor, harmless cleanup, or where the hypothesis does not reproduce.

## 3. Aggregate Performance Metrics

### Deterministic Rules Engine
- **Total Rule Triggers:** 9 rule instances flagged across 10 diffs.
- **Rule 1 (Deleted Assertions):** 4 flagged (3 Higher, 1 Lower [moved assertion correctly cancelled]). 3/3 Higher were genuine coverage regressions. **Precision: 100%**.
- **Rule 2 (Empty Catch Handlers):** 4 flagged. 3 were genuine swallowed errors; 1 was harmless analytics logging. **Precision: 75.0%**.
- **Rule 3 (Skipped / Focused Tests):** 2 flagged (`it.skip` and `describe.only`). Both were regressions blocking test execution. **Precision: 100%**.
- **Overall Deterministic Precision:** **88.9% (8/9)**.

### LLM Hypotheses Tier & Citation Verifier
- **Total LLM Hypotheses Generated:** 11 hypotheses across 10 diffs.
- **Validly Cited Hypotheses:** 9 hypotheses (81.8%).
- **Quarantined Uncited Hypotheses:** 2 hypotheses (18.2%) successfully quarantined in the collapsed drawer (1 adversarial synthetic citation, 1 hallucinated line).
- **Verified Cited True Regressions:** 6 of 9 cited hypotheses reproduced as true regressions.
- **LLM Cited Precision:** **66.7% (6/9)**.
- **Quarantine Accuracy:** **100% (2/2)** of invalid citations correctly rejected by client-side Citation Verifier.

---

## 4. Key Takeaways & Epistemic Separation

1. **Facts vs. Hypotheses:** The deterministic tier provided 100% repeatable, lightning-fast regression alerts (< 2ms) that never hallucinated. The LLM tier provided deeper architectural insights (like the `jwt.decode` security bypass) but occasionally generated unverifiable claims.
2. **The SentLineIds Constraint Works:** The Citation Verifier completely prevented hallucinated line citations from cluttering the primary review surface, isolating them into the collapsed uncited drawer.
3. **Honest Empty State:** On clean diffs (Diff 06), ChangeGuard's refusal to declare the diff "safe" prevented false confidence: *"No findings. This does not mean the diff is safe. Most real regressions depend on repository context outside this diff."*
