How ChangeGuard Works

A complete guide to the codebase, data flow, and epistemic verification engine.

System Overview

ChangeGuard is an honest, offline-first regression reviewer for AI-generated JS/TS diffs. Instead of blending facts and model guesses, it enforces an epistemic boundary: deterministic rules verify facts directly in the diff, while LLM hypotheses are held to strict client-side citation verification against sentLineIds.

Code Tour Route (4 Stops)

Stop 1 · The Foundation
Unified Diff Parser & Global Line Indexer
src/core/parser.ts → parseDiff()
Parses hunks, normalizes a/ and b/ paths, truncates lines > 2,000 characters, and assigns immutable global IDs (L0001, L0002...) that remain stable across all tiers.
Stop 2 · Deterministic Facts Engine
The Three Code Hygiene Rules with Move Matching
src/core/rules/ → runAllRules(), evaluateRule1()
Scans for deleted test assertions, introduced empty error handlers, and skipped/focused tests. Uses move-matcher.ts to match moved assertions 1-to-1 and downgrade them from Higher to Lower likelihood.
Stop 3 · The Epistemic Gatekeeper
Dynamic Citation Verifier & Schema Validator
src/core/verifier.ts → verifyCitations() & src/core/schema.ts
Evaluates LLM findings against sentLineIds. Valid citations with at least one changed line are marked Cited; hallucinations or out-of-diff lines are quarantined into the collapsed Uncited drawer.
Stop 4 · Dual-Pane Interactive UI
Unified Diff Viewer & Evidence Ledger
src/ui/DiffViewer.tsx & src/ui/EvidenceLedger.tsx
Coordinates bi-directional line jumping, flashing highlights, source-colored gutter dots (steel-blue vs. amber), and renders the honest empty state: "No findings. This does not mean the diff is safe."

A Practice to Reuse on Future Projects

Epistemic Separation of Model Output

Never let an LLM self-report its own certainty or ground its own citations without mechanical verification. By enforcing that citations must be line IDs checked against sentLineIds in code, hallucinations are caught by construction before reaching developers.
